## Research

Six approved entries in the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences are credited to William Alexander Carney. Five come from exact computation: I authored A398582 and A398691 (Erdős problem #327), extended A390394 (#301) and A392164 (#1109) by integer linear programming and exact branch-and-bound, and added new terms to A386620 from an exact residue-class search. One A390394 witness carries a Lean 4 kernel-checked certificate. A386620 links C++ and Python programs.

I also authored A397714, a constant associated with the Reeb–Wolf entropy bound.

My current research is the mathematics of efficient local LLM inference: joint weight and KV-cache precision allocation, correlation-aware attention-error certificates, and exact interval computation of quantized model losses. No capability gain is claimed until held-out measurements exist.

## How I work

- **Agents build, gates decide.** I use builders and independent reviewers, including cross-vendor audits, to verify agent-produced work.
- **Humans approve consequential actions.** Consequential actions by the AI operating team require human approval.
- **Verification over confidence.** I want a command, a test, or a live check behind a claim of "done".
- **Scale.** The engineering platform behind this work has run 30+ named AI agent seats across machines and model vendors (Anthropic, OpenAI, Moonshot, and local models).
